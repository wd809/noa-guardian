# Compañero §3 — USC Título 29 (PDF, ~4.1 MB)

Título completo al release point PL 119-121. Relevancia Citizen (C7): contiene la **Fair Labor Standards Act** (29 U.S.C. §§ 201 y ss.) — salario mínimo federal (§ 206: $7.25/h desde el 24-jul-2009) y horas extra (§ 207: 1.5× tras 40 h/semana). Es la pieza federal que, combinada con la § 4(a.1) de la Minimum Wage Act de PA (ver `../pa/salarios-pa-mwa-wpcl.md`), sostiene la tarifa aplicable en PA.

## Verificación fina FLSA §§ 206/207 — CERRADA (2026-10-22, pasada C8)

Los pasajes exactos se localizaron y leyeron **dentro del PDF oficial** `usc29@119-121.pdf` (Cap. 8, Fair Labor Standards), y su vigencia se contrastó con las leyes públicas posteriores al release point (ver nota de re-verificación arriba). Texto operativo verificado, palabra por palabra:

- **§ 206(a)(1)** — «Every employer shall pay to each of his employees who in any workweek is engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, or is employed in an enterprise engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, wages at the following rates: (1) except as otherwise provided in this section, not less than— (A) $5.85 an hour, beginning on the 60th day after May 25, 2007; (B) $6.55 an hour, beginning 12 months after that 60th day; and **(C) $7.25 an hour, beginning 24 months after that 60th day**» (= 24-jul-2009; la afirmación «$7.25/h desde el 24-jul-2009» de este compañero es exacta).
- **§ 207(a)(1)** — «no employer shall employ any of his employees who in any workweek is engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, or is employed in an enterprise engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, **for a workweek longer than forty hours unless such employee receives compensation for his employment in excess of the hours above specified at a rate not less than one and one-half times the regular rate** at which he is employed» (la afirmación «1.5× tras 40 h/semana» es exacta).
- **Sin cambios posteriores aplicables:** las leyes públicas promulgadas tras el corte del release point hasta el 2026-10-22 (PL 119-122, T. 25; PL 119-123, T. 16; PL 119-124, ERISA § 3(18)) no enmiendan los §§ 206/207 ni el Cap. 8 — escaneo de texto completo de las tres leyes en govinfo (GPO), 0 menciones a la FLSA. Con esto **C7 queda cerrada también a nivel federal**: la derivación de la tarifa PA ($7.25 vía MWA § 4(a.1) + FLSA § 206) descansa en texto verificado en la fuente oficial.
